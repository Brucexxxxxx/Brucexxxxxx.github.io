# About Me

Hi, I'm Bruce, and I'm interested in taking something messy and helping it become clear.

Challenges shape me more than anything else. When things get hard, that's usually when I learn the most about who I am and what I can actually do.

What excites me is having a clear goal to work toward. I don't do well with vague, open ended stuff. When I know exactly what I'm building and why, I get motivated to actually finish it.

 Right now my goal is to build a great website. I don't have the idea for it yet though.
